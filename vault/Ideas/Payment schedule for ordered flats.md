# Payment schedule for ordered flats

## What

For a lead at the «Захиалга» stage: the agreed price, down payment, instalments with due dates, and what has been paid, with overdue payments on the dashboard.

## Why it matters

After the order the sales team tracks money in spreadsheets; keeping it next to the lead and flat closes the loop from first visit to keys.

## Notes

- One table of payments per lead; the agreed price would come from the flat's price on the day it was sold.
